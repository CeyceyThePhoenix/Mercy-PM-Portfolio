Case Study · Fintech
NovaPay
Problem

65% of Sub-Saharan Africans are denied formal credit not because they're poor risks, but because traditional bureau scoring was never designed for gig workers and mobile money users.

Solution

NovaPay built a Buy Now, Pay Later product that uses 12+ months of mobile money behavioural data — income regularity, savings patterns, merchant spend — to generate an AI credit score in 60 seconds with no collateral required.

Outcome

Responsible credit access delivered to Africa's 4M+ underbanked mobile money users, unlocking a $420B BNPL market while replacing 40–120% APR moneylender debt with fair, structured repayment.

Case Study · Agritech
FarmConnect
Problem

Nigeria's 36M+ smallholder farmers are trapped in poverty because they lack access to verified inputs, formal credit, and fair markets for their harvests.

Solution

FarmConnect built a USSD-first platform combining an input marketplace, digital wallet, weather-indexed crop insurance, and offtaker matching — all accessible without a smartphone.

Outcome

150,000 farmers onboarded by Month 12, ₦4B in GMV generated, post-harvest losses reduced by 30%, and farmer income lifted by 25%.

Industry Insights & Takes
Short, Sharp Perspectives

On product, health tech, and African innovation.

Why "Move Fast & Break Things" Fails in Healthcare

Healthcare is not a sandbox. When you ship a buggy feature in a fintech app, users lose money. When you do it in health tech, users lose lives. The right PM mindset for health products isn't slow — it's precise. Rapid iteration must be paired with clinical validation, regulatory awareness, and deep empathy for vulnerable users. Speed without safety is just recklessness.

USSD Is Not a Compromise — It's a Strategy

Years of teaching taught me how to truly listen. I know how to find the real problem underneath the stated one — and how to design solutions that meet people where they actually are, not where we assume they are.

The PM Skill Nobody Talks About: Clinical Thinking

Doctors and pharmacists are trained to gather symptoms, form hypotheses, test interventions, and measure outcomes. Sound familiar? That's product management. Clinical thinking gives us a structured framework to run discovery, define problems, and ship solutions without the chaos of gut-feel product decisions.

Product Philosophy
A Few Principles That Guide Every Decision

Shaped by field research, and hard-won lessons from building in emerging markets.

Evidence Over Intuition

Every feature starts with a question, not an assumption. I run discovery before I run sprints.

Constraints Are Creative

Low bandwidth, feature phones, and limited literacy aren't blockers — they're design briefs. The best solutions emerge from real constraints.

Ship, Measure, Iterate

A good product shipped beats a perfect product planned. I bias toward action, then let data guide the next move.

Users Are Not Edge Cases

Rural patients, low-income users, and non-English speakers are not edge cases — they're the core use case. I design for them first.

Point of View
How I Think About Product

"The Layer Nobody Talks About"

Before the strategy. Before the wireframe. Before the roadmap — there is data. And most product teams are too far upstream to ever really understand it. I came up through that layer, and it changed how I see everything. Good products aren't designed from the top down. They're understood from the bottom up.

"The Real Job of a PM"

A product manager's job isn't to have the best ideas. It's to create the conditions where the best ideas can be found, tested, and shipped. The best PMs aren't the loudest voices in the room. They're the ones asking the question nobody else thought to ask.

"What Teaching Taught Me About Users"

People rarely tell you their real problem. You have to earn that through listening, not asking. I learned that as a teacher — and it's the most useful thing I bring into product. The real insight is always one layer deeper than the first answer.
